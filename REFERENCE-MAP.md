# Reference and originality map

The public PITAKA storefront was inspected for high-level ecommerce rhythm only: product-led heroes, category and product discovery, restrained typography, editorial lifestyle transitions, clear collection grids and purchase controls. Apple's iPhone product page informed the product-page anchor navigation, clear purchase area and long-form visual storytelling rhythm. These are structural references, not a request to copy source code, exact assets, trademarked design details or copy.

| Lumasela area | Reference principle | Lumasela implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Large product-led opening with concise hierarchy | Original Lumasela imagery, English copy and responsive desktop/mobile art direction |
| Category discovery | Compact, image-led entry points into product families | Four editable cards with independent original visuals and links |
| Product showcase | Product cards connected directly to commerce data | Selected Shopify collection, real prices and standard Dawn quick add; clearly labelled preview cards when no public collection is connected |
| Signature styles | Product comparison cards | Four original editable signature treatments over a shared clear-case concept visual |
| Customizer | Clear option-to-purchase path | Original form controls and live preview; Shopify line-item properties when connected to a product |
| Lifestyle grid | Alternating editorial pacing | Original mirror, city and desk scenes with independent HTML copy |
| Collection | Native commerce grid, filters and collection-specific context | Dynamic collection image/title/description plus Dawn/Shopify products, filters, sorting, quick add and responsive cards |
| Product | Product sub-navigation, clear purchase controls and visual feature storytelling | Dawn product form plus Lumasela anchor navigation, customizer, editable feature facts, distinct lifestyle/detail images, FAQ and related products |
| Service information | Clear operational reassurance close to decisions | Factual MYR, Shopify checkout, compatibility and contact reminders; no unverified shipping or warranty promises |

The reference storefront also displays press/review claims. Lumasela does not yet have verified publication quotes or customer review data, so that module is intentionally not fabricated. It can be added when attributable content is supplied.

No PITAKA source code, logo, copy, proprietary product imagery or product texture is included. The included device/case visuals remain concept artwork until the merchant provides verified production assets.
