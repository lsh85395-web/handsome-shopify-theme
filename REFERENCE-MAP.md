# Askel reference map for Handsome

Inspected on 2026-10-21 in desktop (1280px) and mobile (390px) browsers: home, men's collection, Glide product, cart, Our Story, FAQ, and Contact.

| Reference | Handsome implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Overlay header, desktop dropdowns, mobile drawer, search/cart | Dawn header with Shopify navigation, search and cart |
| Full-bleed shoe hero and angled accent | Handsome hero with desktop/mobile images and two buttons |
| Find your pair and repeated product rails | Handsome product rail using Shopify collections and product records |
| Brand statement and material benefits | Handsome statement and features |
| Product story, hotspot media and spec bar | Handsome editorial, hotspots, specs |
| Testimonials | Editorial notes; no invented customer reviews |
| Accessories and gym promotions | Repeatable product rail and editorial section |
| Before/after slider | Handsome comparison with merchant images |
| Newsletter and footer | Shopify newsletter form and Dawn footer |
| Collection header, category chips, filters/grid | Collection banner, category links, native filters/sorting/grid |
| Product gallery, variant, quantity, cart | Dawn product form connected to Shopify product and inventory |
| Story, FAQ, support, contact | Editable default-page router for the four Handsome page handles, Shopify contact form, and optional dedicated JSON templates |

Visual differences: original generated photography, original Handsome mark and English copy; no Taiga source, paid fonts, product imagery, testimonials, claims or policies reused. Reference collection/product features such as quick-buy modals and fit scales are approximated with native product links and variant selection.

Only source-backed facts are represented as such. Merchant must replace demo product records, descriptions, prices, stock and policy content in Shopify before accepting orders.
