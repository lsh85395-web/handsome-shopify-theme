# Lumasela Shopify theme

An editable Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme for Lumasela's clear phone case and signature customization concept. It is built on Shopify's Dawn 16.0.0 foundation and keeps the Dawn license in `LICENSE.md`.

## Included storefront work

- English homepage with responsive hero, category discovery, signature styles, live design preview, dynamic Shopify product showcase, lifestyle, product detail, brand story, gift and trust sections. Distinct original campaign images are used across the page rather than repeating one visual.
- A Lumasela wordmark lockup is rendered in the header when the merchant has not uploaded a logo image; a standalone SVG logo is also included.
- Editable collection template with a per-collection hero, native Shopify filtering, sorting, quick add and real product data.
- Dawn's production product template plus optional `product.lumasela` template with a product anchor bar, Shopify purchase controls, personalization, editable feature specifications, editorial storytelling, FAQ, related products and trust information.
- Signature name, style, color and position are separate HTML controls. When the Lumasela product template is connected to a real product, the values are submitted as cart line-item properties.
- About, FAQ and Support page templates are included. Navigation and footer links use Shopify menus so the merchant can maintain them without code.
- All marketing copy, links, images, repeated cards and section order are editable in the theme editor. Product titles, prices, media, variants, inventory and collection membership remain Shopify product data.

## Important status

- The included iPhone 18 Pro imagery is a **concept design**, not a claim of official Apple hardware or production-verified case fit.
- No material, drop-test, anti-yellowing, magnetic, manufacturing-time, delivery or warranty claims are asserted.
- The connected Shopify store currently has three `VAULTA` demonstration phone-case products in **Draft** status. They are not verified Lumasela products and cannot provide a production-ready public Lumasela catalog. This theme does not modify or publish product records.
- The homepage customizer therefore starts in clearly labelled design-preview mode. Connect a verified phone-case product in the theme editor before using it for orders.
- Shipping rates, taxes, markets, payment providers, legal policies and domain settings are store-level configuration and are intentionally not changed by the theme.
- Do not publish the preview theme without the store owner's explicit approval.
